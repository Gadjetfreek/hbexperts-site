HBE INTERNAL · EXERCISE BUYER 001

Donald Kelley · Consultation Brief

Purpose: Understand Donald and his partner well enough to decide together whether HBE should represent them. Do not repeat Discovery questions merely because they are on a form.

Donald at a glance

  • Exploring a downsizing / lifestyle change
  • Roughly 3–6 month horizon
  • Medina focus
  • Currently owns; expects to sell before buying
  • Working with a lender
  • Wants data and weighs options carefully before committing
  • Success, in his words: “Getting the right home.”

What already seems important

  • Quality over sheer size
  • Kitchen as a gathering place
  • Outdoor living
  • Space that works for regular entertaining
  • Suburban/community setting
  • Church/community nearby
  • Basement required
  • Open to the right project home

Questions worth exploring

  • What does downsizing need to make better for you and your partner?
  • When you say lifestyle change, what do you hope everyday life looks like afterward?
  • What draws you specifically to Medina?
  • What does “quality over quantity” mean when you walk into a home?
  • How much project work would feel interesting rather than burdensome?
  • What makes a kitchen work for the way you gather and entertain?
  • What kind of outdoor living would you actually use?
  • You marked a basement as required. What makes that important?
  • How firm is selling before buying, and what would make that sequence comfortable?
  • You favored the “perfect price” tradeoff. What would you be willing to compromise to get there?
  • Picture an ordinary Saturday morning in the right next home. What are you doing?
  • Beyond the property itself, how will you know you found “the right home”?

Things HBE should explain

  • What exclusive buyer representation means in plain English
  • How HBE works differently from a traditional buyer agent
  • How compensation works
  • How we research, evaluate and challenge a property before recommending action
  • That Donald is never obligated to rush to the next checkpoint

Do not ask again

Do not make Donald restate basic facts already captured above unless clarification is genuinely useful. Reference what he already told us and ask the next better question.

Capture before ending

  • Donald's deeper WHY, preferably in his words
  • Anything that changed from Discovery
  • Confirmed dealbreakers and meaningful flex points
  • What HBE promised to do next
  • Whether Donald wants to move forward with HBE
