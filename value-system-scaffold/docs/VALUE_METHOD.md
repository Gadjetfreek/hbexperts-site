# VALUE Method

## Definition

**VALUE = Values · Alternatives · Learning · Uncertainty · Evidence**

**Human-centered homebuying decision support.**

> Price tells you what a home costs. VALUE helps you decide what that cost means to you.

## Purpose

VALUE exists to improve the conditions under which a buyer makes a consequential housing decision.

It does not claim to know the objectively correct home for a person.

It helps the buyer see the decision more clearly, preserve meaningful agency, and revise the answer as new information appears.

## The Five Questions

### Values
What matters to the buyer and why?

### Alternatives
What other choices remain possible?

### Learning
What has this experience changed in our understanding?

### Uncertainty
What remains unknown, and what would it mean if our assumption is wrong?

### Evidence
What facts, comparisons, professional findings, and firsthand experience support the current view?

## Recurrence

VALUE should recur:

- after meaningful journey stages
- after each showing
- before an offer
- when negotiation materially changes the deal
- after inspections/due diligence
- when value/appraisal evidence changes
- when financing materially changes
- before final commitment

## Multi-Buyer Principle

A household is one shared journey composed of individual humans.

Preserve each person's voice first. Correlate second.

Do not average meaningful differences away.

## Decision Record

VALUE should create a living record of:

- what mattered
- what changed
- what was learned
- what remained uncertain
- what evidence mattered
- which alternatives were considered
- what decision was made and why

## Conflict Standard

The adopting brokerage should attempt to:

1. eliminate conflicts where practical
2. reduce/neutralize what cannot be eliminated
3. disclose what remains
4. preserve informed choice
5. monitor for drift
6. decline/withdraw where the conflict cannot be responsibly managed

## Prohibited Uses

VALUE must not be repurposed to score or infer:

- likelihood to close
- willingness to overpay
- persuasion susceptibility
- objection resistance
- emotional vulnerability
- urgency sensitivity

It must not use protected-class information or proxies to steer buyers.

## Success

A showing is successful when it improves understanding, even if the property is ruled out.

A stage is successful when the buyer is better equipped to choose the next step, even if the next step is pause, change direction, or stop.

A transaction is not success if it is wrong for the buyer.
