# VALUE

**Values · Alternatives · Learning · Uncertainty · Evidence**

**Human-centered homebuying decision support.**

> Price tells you what a home costs. VALUE helps you decide what that cost means to you.

VALUE is a brokerage-agnostic decision-support system designed for Exclusive Buyer Agency practices.

It is intended to be configured and presented by the local EBA brokerage using it:

> **VALUE**  
> Human-centered homebuying decision support.  
> Brought to you by **{{BROKERAGE_NAME}}**, serving homebuyers for **{{BROKERAGE_YEARS}} years**.

VALUE does not replace the brokerage, broker, agent, buyer, or licensed professional judgment.

The brokerage retains its own:

- legal identity
- licenses
- service area
- history
- branding
- compensation model
- contracts
- local market expertise
- compliance obligations
- client relationships

VALUE supplies a shared decision-support method and software architecture.

## Core Structure

VALUE asks five recurring questions throughout the homebuying journey:

1. **Values** — What matters to this buyer, and why?
2. **Alternatives** — What other choices remain available?
3. **Learning** — What has this stage, showing, or new fact taught us?
4. **Uncertainty** — What remains unknown, and how much does it matter?
5. **Evidence** — What facts, comparisons, professional findings, and lived experience support the decision?

The system does not generate a hidden purchase score or tell the buyer what to choose.

## Product Views

The buyer experience is organized around three connected views:

- **Journey** — Where are we in the process?
- **Showings** — What have the homes taught us?
- **Decision Record** — What have we learned about the decision and ourselves?

For multi-buyer households, each person has an individual identity and reflection layer while shared facts, showings, dates, and household decisions remain connected.

## Non-Negotiable Principle

The buyer remains the chooser.

Technology may organize evidence, surface questions, preserve learning, and help identify tradeoffs. It must not quietly become a persuasion engine designed to increase closing probability.
