## What It Is

The Buyer Strategy Session is a **complimentary**, about **one-hour** conversation — in person when possible, or by Zoom. There is **no cost, no obligation, and no sales pressure**.

Sending a Buyer Experience does not hire HomeBuyer Experts, sign an agency agreement, or obligate you to buy a home.

## What You Get

Most searches start with “What homes are available?” We start with “What are you trying to accomplish, and what would help you choose well?”

We use VALUE as a simple structure:

- **Values** — what matters and why
- **Alternatives** — what other paths remain possible
- **Learning** — what you already know
- **Uncertainty** — what unanswered questions could matter
- **Evidence** — what information would make the decision clearer

Together we talk through your why, priorities, decision style, tradeoffs, timing and financing context, risks, and questions — including ones you may not know to ask yet.

## What Happens Next

1. **Explore the Buyer Journey** — see the process before sharing anything with HBE.
2. **Start My Buyer Experience when you are ready** — draft privately on your device. Reflective answers are optional. When ready, **Review & Send to HomeBuyer Experts**. Each buyer answers individually.
3. **We review before we meet** — so we do not spend your time re-asking what you already shared.
4. **We have a real conversation** — clarify what matters, what options remain, and what evidence would help.

The next useful step may be to search — or to prepare, learn, change the plan, or wait. You can pause a draft anytime and resume later through **Open my Buyer Portal**.

## Fair Housing and No Steering

HomeBuyer Experts works for buyers and follows Fair Housing law. We do not steer buyers toward or away from neighborhoods based on protected characteristics. Representation helps you choose wisely — it does not decide for you.

During the session we can also explain exclusive buyer representation and the **Buyer Value Agreement** — what representation means, how compensation works, and what each party is committing to. Nothing is signed merely because we talked.
