## Why Start With Strategy?

Most buyers begin with: *"What homes are available?"*

Successful buyers begin with: *"What is the smartest way to approach this purchase?"*

The Buyer Strategy Session helps answer:

- What should you know before making offers?
- How should you approach negotiations?
- What risks should you avoid?
- How can you protect your financial interests?

---

## What We Discuss

### Your Goals
Why are you moving? What matters most? What does success look like?

### Your Market Position
Budget, timeline, location, and competition in today's market.

### Your Purchase Strategy
Representation options, negotiation approach, and decision-making process.

### Your Questions
No pressure. Just expert guidance.
