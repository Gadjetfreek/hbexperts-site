## A Complimentary, No-Obligation Conversation

The Buyer Strategy Session is **complimentary** — no cost, no obligation, no pressure.

It runs approximately **one hour** and is held **in person** whenever possible. Zoom is also available.

This is not a sales appointment. It is a working conversation about a consequential decision.

Sending a Buyer Experience does not hire HomeBuyer Experts, sign an agency agreement, or obligate you to buy a home.

---

## Why Start With Strategy?

Most home searches begin with: *"What homes are available?"*

We prefer to begin with: *"What are you actually trying to accomplish, and what would help you choose well?"*

VALUE gives that conversation a simple structure:

- **Values** — what matters and why
- **Alternatives** — what other paths remain possible
- **Learning** — what you already know and what experience may teach us
- **Uncertainty** — what concerns or unanswered questions could matter
- **Evidence** — what information would make the decision clearer

> **Price tells you what a home costs. VALUE helps you decide what that cost means to you.**

Before the search gains momentum, we want to understand:

- What has you considering a move now?
- What would a good outcome mean in your own words?
- Which priorities are genuinely important, and which are flexible?
- What worries you most?
- How do you tend to make major decisions?
- What information helps you feel informed rather than overwhelmed?
- What tradeoffs are you willing to make?
- What would cause you to pause or walk away?

## What We Discuss

### Your Why
What is happening in your life that has made housing a decision now?

### Your Values
What should this move improve in your life? Which priorities are fundamental, and which may change as you learn?

### Your Decision Style
Do you tend to research deeply, decide quickly, talk choices through, need time, or want information organized in a particular way? There is no correct style. Knowing yours helps us serve you better.

### Alternatives and Tradeoffs
What options exist besides the first obvious path? What are you willing to exchange for something that matters more?

### Your Market Position
Budget, financing, timing, location, current market conditions, and realistic alternatives.

### Risk and Uncertainty
What do we know? What do we not know? Which unknowns matter enough to investigate before moving forward?

### Evidence
What information, comparisons, records, professional input, or firsthand experience would help you make a more informed choice?

### Your Questions
You do not need to know the right questions in advance. Part of our job is helping uncover the questions the decision deserves.

## What Happens Next

**Step 1 — Explore the Buyer Journey.** See the process before sharing anything with HBE.

**Step 2 — Start My Buyer Experience when you are ready.** Draft privately on your device. Reflective answers are optional. When you are ready, **Review & Send to HomeBuyer Experts**. Each buyer answers individually so we can preserve the voices of the people actually making the decision.

**Step 3 — We review before we meet.** We should not spend your time asking questions you have already answered.

**Step 4 — We have a real conversation.** Together we clarify VALUE: what matters, what alternatives remain, what has already been learned, where uncertainty remains, and what evidence would help.

The next useful step may be to search. It may also be to prepare, learn, change the plan, or wait.

You can pause the draft anytime and resume later through login / Open my Buyer Portal.

## VALUE Continues After the Session

The Strategy Session is the starting point, not the last time these questions matter.

After showings and meaningful journey stages, VALUE returns:

- What did we learn?
- Did any value become more or less important?
- What alternatives remain?
- What uncertainty appeared or disappeared?
- What evidence changed the picture?

The Buyer Decision Profile and Decision Record are meant to evolve as you do.

## The Standard We Use

Throughout the conversation, we come back to one question:

> **What is best for the buyer?**

And one practical test:

> **Does this help you choose more wisely?**

VALUE is the repeatable framework we use to help answer that without pretending the system knows your answer for you.

That may ultimately lead toward a purchase. It may also lead toward a different property, a different plan, more information, more time, or no purchase at all.
