# Print Specifications

## Finished Size

5 × 7 inches, portrait

## Review Copies

For ordinary home or office printing:

- Print two workbook pages on each 8.5 × 11-inch sheet.
- Print double-sided when available.
- Cut sheets to size.
- Staple along the left edge or booklet fold.
- Print coloring and activity editions in black and white unless color is
  necessary for review.

## Professional Production Target

- Finished trim: 5 × 7 inches
- Interior: uncoated writing-friendly stock
- Cover: heavier matte stock
- Interior bleed: only where artwork reaches the page edge
- Safe margin: minimum 0.375 inch
- Binding margin: minimum 0.5 inch where required
- Page count: preferably divisible by four for saddle stitching

## Writing Considerations

Interior paper must accept:

- Pencil
- Crayon
- Colored pencil
- Ballpoint pen
- Gel pen

Avoid glossy interior stock.
