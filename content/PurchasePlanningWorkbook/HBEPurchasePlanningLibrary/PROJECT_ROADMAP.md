## Phase 2 — Review Samples

Create two short, printable prototypes for family review.

### Prototype A: Early Writers

Working title: My New Home Adventure

Target length: 12–16 pages

Include:

- Cover
- Welcome page
- This Is Me
- How I Feel About Moving
- What I Love About My Home Now
- Draw a Room for You
- People and Pets Coming With Us
- Things I Hope Are Nearby
- Home Tour Coloring/Observation Page
- My Questions
- Family Conversation Page
- Back cover

### Prototype B: Teen Advisors

Working title: My Voice in the Move

Target length: 16–20 pages

Include:

- Cover
- A direct introduction
- What This Move Changes for Me
- What Adults May Not Realize
- Privacy and Personal Space
- School, Work, Friends, and Transportation
- What I Want Nearby
- What I Can Compromise On
- What I Cannot Easily Compromise On
- Home Tour Scorecard
- Concerns About the Move
- Questions I Want Answered
- Family Discussion Page
- Final Reflection
- Back cover

## Phase 3 — Family Review

Reviewers should consider:

- Does the language feel respectful?
- Does the format feel useful?
- Are any questions uncomfortable or intrusive?
- Does the book allow disagreement?
- Would a child or teenager actually complete it?
- Is the 5 × 7 format large enough for writing?
- Which pages create meaningful discussion?
- Which pages feel like filler?
- What is missing?
