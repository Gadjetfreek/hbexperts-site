# HBE Purchase Planning Library

The HBE Purchase Planning Library helps individuals and families think
clearly before choosing a home.

It is not a collection of sales brochures.

It is a family-centered decision system designed to give every person
affected by a move an appropriate way to participate.

The library includes guided journals for adults, early writers, growing
children, young advisors, and teenagers.

## Core Purpose

A home purchase affects more than finances.

It can change:

- Daily routines
- Friendships
- Schools
- Work and commuting
- Family traditions
- Privacy
- Independence
- Responsibilities
- A person's sense of belonging

Every member of a household may experience those changes differently.

These workbooks create space for those perspectives to be expressed,
heard, and considered.

## Governing Principle

> Every person involved receives a voice, not a predetermined role.

The content must not assume:

- A particular family structure
- Which adult manages money
- Traditional gender roles
- That all children feel excited about moving
- That every household includes children
- That every reader has the same writing or reading ability
- That agreement is required for a person's feelings to be valid

## Product Format

The primary format is a compact 5 × 7-inch guided journal.

The books should feel personal and lasting—not like real estate paperwork.

They should be:

- Easy to carry during home tours
- Comfortable for children to hold
- Suitable for writing or drawing
- Printable at home for review copies
- Adaptable for professional booklet printing
- Visually related across the full library

## First Review Set

The initial review set will contain:

### My New Home Adventure

For the youngest readers who can write short words or sentences.

The sample will include:

- Drawing and coloring areas
- Simple emotional check-ins
- A room-design activity
- A neighborhood observation activity
- Space to identify hopes and worries

### My Voice in the Move

For teenagers.

The sample will include:

- Honest acknowledgment that moving may be unwanted
- Independence, privacy, school, work, and transportation questions
- A home-tour scorecard
- Space for concerns adults may overlook
- A family discussion page
