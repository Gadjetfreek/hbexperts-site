# HBE Purchase Planning Library Design System

## Product Character

The library should feel like a family of compact field journals.

Desired qualities:

- Calm
- Thoughtful
- Warm
- Intelligent
- Durable
- Personal
- Uncluttered

The design should not resemble:

- A school worksheet packet
- A coloring book sold as a distraction
- A mortgage application
- A corporate sales brochure
- A childish activity book

## Trim Size

Primary finished size:

5 inches wide × 7 inches tall

## Page Orientation

Portrait

## Binding Assumption

Design first for:

1. Saddle-stitched review booklet
2. Perfect-bound finished edition
3. Digital PDF viewed one page at a time

Important content should remain away from the inside binding edge.

## Page Rhythm

Use a mixture of:

- Guided reflection pages
- Drawing pages
- Short educational pages
- Check-in pages
- Home-tour observation pages
- Family conversation pages
- Open note pages

Avoid placing dense prompts on every page.

White space is part of the experience.

## Shared Visual Language

All editions should share:

- HBE identity
- Consistent heading system
- Common icons
- Related cover structure
- Page numbers
- Edition identifier
- A recognizable home or doorway motif

## Age-Appropriate Adaptation

### Early Writers

- Large type
- Short directions
- One principal activity per page
- Large drawing areas
- Simple icons
- Minimal writing demands

### Growing Explorers

- Short paragraphs
- Checkboxes and scales
- Maps, scavenger activities, and comparisons
- Moderate writing areas

### Young Advisors

- Observation tools
- Ranking activities
- Early trade-off exercises
- More nuanced emotional vocabulary

### Teen Advisors

- Mature typography
- Minimal cartoon imagery
- Direct writing
- Privacy and independence prompts
- Space for disagreement
- Practical decision tools

## Accessibility

- Do not communicate meaning through color alone.
- Maintain strong text contrast.
- Use readable type sizes.
- Avoid overly decorative fonts.
- Provide alternatives to drawing-based answers.
- Permit checkmarks, words, sentences, or sketches.
