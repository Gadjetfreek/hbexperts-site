We work only for home buyers — never for the seller.

You can see the entire Buyer Journey before providing HBE any personal information. You can explore every stage before sharing personal information. Nothing goes to HBE until you deliberately review and send a Buyer Experience.

## What the Buyer Journey is

The Buyer Journey is how you can see the process HomeBuyer Experts uses to help you understand the homes, the risks, the tradeoffs, and your choices before you make a major commitment.

Sometimes the best choice is to move forward. Sometimes it is to negotiate differently, change the search, wait, or walk away.

## Our North Star

> **What is best for the buyer?**

A home purchase can affect your money, family, time, work, and future for years. Good representation should make the decision clearer — not push you to decide faster.

We help you understand your options, investigate important questions, protect your leverage, and make a choice you can own.

## How it works

We keep returning to what matters as you learn.

1. **Understand You** — Learn what matters and how you like to decide.
2. **Clarify the Choice** — Make priorities, options, unknowns, and tradeoffs visible.
3. **Learn the Market** — Use current facts to see what your options really are.
4. **Evaluate Homes** — Treat each property as new information — not just a checklist.
5. **Negotiate Carefully** — Protect leverage and match price and terms to your goals.
6. **Recheck Before Commit** — As facts change, ask whether this is still the choice you want.

## VALUE

Price shows the cost. VALUE helps you decide what it means.

VALUE is a simple way to think through a home choice: **Values · Alternatives · Learning · Uncertainty · Evidence.** VALUE does not decide for you. You do.
