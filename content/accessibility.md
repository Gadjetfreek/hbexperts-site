## Our Commitment

HomeBuyer Experts believes that everyone deserves access to clear, useful information — regardless of ability or the technology they use to browse the web.

We are committed to ensuring that this website is accessible to people with disabilities, and we actively work to meet recognized accessibility standards.

---

## Standards We Follow

This website strives to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA** — an internationally recognized standard for digital accessibility developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

WCAG 2.1 AA addresses accessibility across four principles:

- **Perceivable** — Information is presented in ways all users can perceive
- **Operable** — Interface components and navigation are operable by all users
- **Understandable** — Content and operation are clear and predictable
- **Robust** — Content works reliably with current and future assistive technologies

---

## What We've Done

In building and maintaining this site, we have focused on:

- Sufficient color contrast for readability
- Descriptive text for links and navigation
- Logical heading structure for screen reader navigation
- Keyboard-navigable interface elements
- Responsive design that works across device types and screen sizes
- Meaningful alt text for images where applicable

## Known Limitations

We are a small team and continually improving. There may be areas of the site that do not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. We are committed to identifying and addressing gaps over time.
