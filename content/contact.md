## What a Good First Conversation Should Do

A useful conversation should leave you with more clarity than you started with.

That might mean:

- understanding an option you did not know you had;
- identifying a risk worth investigating;
- seeing a tradeoff more clearly;
- recognizing that you need more time or information;
- or deciding that the next step is worth taking.

Our job is not to create urgency. It is to help you understand the decision well enough to choose your next step for yourself.

## Our Standard

We weigh our advice against one question:

> **What is best for the buyer?**

And one practical test:

> **Does this help the buyer choose more wisely?**

If the answer is to proceed, we help you proceed well. If the answer is to pause, change direction, or walk away, that is part of good representation too.
